Managed data-center services should run on discipline, not heroics. Here is the checklist we run every quarter for the sites we operate under our [data-center maintenance](/services/hardware/data-center-maintenance) retainer.

## Physical

- **Cage tour.** Photos of every rack, front and back, dated.
- **Cable hygiene.** Every patch labeled at both ends, colors matching the standard.
- **Airflow.** Blanking panels where they should be; no hot air recirculating.
- **PDU headroom.** Each PDU at less than 70% sustained.

## Change and access

- **Change log review.** Every change ticketed, reviewed, and closed.
- **Access review.** Who has cage access, still needed?
- **Vendor escort log.** Every visitor and the ticket that justified them.

## Firmware and CVE

- **Firmware inventory.** Current version vs. vendor stable, per device. Also feeds into the wider [infrastructure support](/services/hardware/infrastructure-support) baseline for the client.
- **CVE exposure.** New CVEs for any device in scope; remediation date planned.
- **Backup verification.** Config backups exist *and* have been restored to a lab in the last quarter.

## Monitoring

- **Alert quality review.** Which alerts fired, which were noise, tune.
- **SLA report.** Response and resolution vs. the SLA, per priority.

## Reporting

- **Written report to the client.** What we did, what we found, what we recommend for next quarter.

If any of these are new to you, that's not a criticism it's a small change window from where you are. For sites moving between facilities, pair this checklist with our [rollout and migration playbook](/services/hardware/rollout-migrations) rather than treating the move as a separate project.
