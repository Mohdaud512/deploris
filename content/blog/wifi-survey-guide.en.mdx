If your business runs on WiFi and your last "survey" was a walkthrough with a phone, this one's for you.

## The three surveys, in order

1. **Predictive.** A software model of the space with your APs placed in it. Cheap, fast, catches obvious mistakes before hardware ships.
2. **Passive on-site.** A quiet walk-through measuring the actual radio environment. Tells you what your neighbors and interference are doing to your real signal.
3. **Post-install validation.** After the APs are up, walk it again against your device mix and SLAs. This is the one people skip; it's the only one that answers "does it actually work."

## What to demand in a deliverable

- Heatmaps for **signal strength** *and* **SNR** not just one.
- Channel plan with a clear rationale.
- Interference report naming the sources.
- BOM sized to actual load, not the vendor's default.
- Roaming plan for the devices you care about (phones, scanners, kiosks).

## The single most common failure

Deploying enough APs but at the wrong power. WiFi is a two-way conversation; if your APs shout and your devices whisper, throughput dies. Passive surveys catch this; walkthroughs don't.
